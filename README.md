
# My Favorite Movie: Minions

> "Bello!" - The Minions

## About the Movie
**Minions** is an animated comedy by *Illumination* and a spin-off of the **Despicable Me** franchise. It follows the Minions, small yellow creatures who spend their lives looking for the most villainous master to serve.

## Main Characters
1. **Kevin**: The tall, responsible leader of the group.
2. **Stuart**: The fun-loving, laid-back Minion who plays the ukulele.
3. **Bob**: The small, innocent Minion with two different-colored eyes.

## Why I Love It
